Beta service terms

AFRICA ATONA is an initial navigation beta. Route estimates are based on configured road routing and do not include a verified live traffic feed. Coverage and accuracy depend on the selected providers. Community reports are unverified and are not emergency dispatch.

Follow road signs, local rules and actual conditions. Make changes and reports only when safely parked. Do not submit personal information about other people or false reports. A selected language or voice does not imply a complete translation or verified spoken navigation support.

Accounts and user-submitted information may be removed for misuse. You can delete your own account from Account. See Privacy for data handling. Third-party map and routing data retain their respective licenses and attribution.
