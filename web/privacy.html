Privacy

Location remains on your device except coordinates sent with road reports or route requests. Searches go to the configured geocoder; map tiles load from OpenStreetMap and reveal your IP address and viewed area. Leaflet and fonts load from external CDNs.

The server stores your account email, display name, salted password hash, expiring sessions, saved places, planned drives and submitted road reports. No continuous GPS trail is recorded. Reports are publicly visible for two hours, and expired records are removed by daily maintenance. Backups rotate after seven days.

Interface and sound preferences are saved in your browser. Device voices depend on your operating system. Account → Delete my account removes associated active data; backup copies may remain for seven days. Clear browser site data to erase local preferences.

The Android app requests foreground location only. Navigation stops when the app is paused. No background location permission is requested.

Questions or data requests: dobcalldemo@gmail.com.

Effective October 19, 2026.
